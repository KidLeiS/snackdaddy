# Snackdaddy logo assets

## Palette

- Snackdaddy Yellow: `#FFD400`
- Snackdaddy Black: `#10110E`

## Two-font system

- **Geist** is the primary family for body copy, navigation, and calls to action.
- **Barlow Condensed** is the display family for the BR-OATS product mark, headlines, numbers, labels, and high-impact campaign copy.
- The rounded Snackdaddy wordmark is fixed custom brand lettering—not an additional typeface for page copy.
- Use weight, scale, and case for hierarchy. Do not introduce additional display or body typefaces.
- Logo files contain outlined letterforms, so no font installation is required to use them.

## Usage

- Use `snackdaddy-wordmark-black` on yellow or pale backgrounds.
- Use `snackdaddy-wordmark-yellow` on black backgrounds.
- Use the `snackdaddy-mark` files for favicons, avatars, and square placements.
- Use `br-oats-wordmark-black` on yellow packaging or campaign backgrounds.
- Use `br-oats-lockup-reversed` when the product descriptor must travel with the logo.
- Use the transparent SVG or PNG wordmarks over site and packaging backgrounds; reserve reversed lockups and square marks for placements that need their own field.
- Keep clear space around each logo equal to at least the height of the full stop in the Snackdaddy wordmark.
- Do not stretch, skew, outline, recolour, or add effects to the marks.

SVG files are transparent master assets unless their name includes `reversed` or `mark`. PNG files preserve transparency and are convenience exports for tools that do not accept SVG.
